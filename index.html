Why it matters

Not a spray.
A bond.

Ceramic coating chemically bonds to your glass. Spray-on treatments sit on top and wash away. Here's what that difference means every time it rains.

Spray treatments Clearline ceramic
Lasts 4–8 weeksUp to 12 months
Sits on glass surfaceChemically bonded
Can smear and streakStreak-free clarity
No scratch resistanceLight scratch protection
Inconsistent DIY coverageEven professional coat
Ice still grips glassIce slides off
Better visibility in rain
At highway speed, water beads and clears the windshield without wipers. You feel the difference on your first rainy drive after the visit.
Less glare at night
Road film and dust scatter light. A clean coated surface keeps headlights where they belong — not scattered across your windshield.
Faster winter mornings
Ice can't grip ceramic glass the way it grips bare glass. Frost and light ice slide off with minimal effort — no scraping required.
Glass that stays cleaner
Dirt and bug splatter can't bond to ceramic the way they bond to bare glass. Between washes, your windows simply look better.
